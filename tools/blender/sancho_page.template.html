The figure is BodyKun from "BodyChan – BodyKun, Posable Drawing Figures" by vinchau (BlendSwap 23521), released under CC0. MakerCAD rebuilt it with Blender 4.5: only its 71 deform bones, one wooden body, and its own Wave and Grip (tools/blender/export_bodykun.py).

The code-built Sancho (crates/mk-sancho) stays as the fallback, and Kris's own design will replace this one.
